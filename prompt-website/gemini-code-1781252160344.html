The thesis

We invest in conviction, hold for decades, and build alongside the founders, families, and operators we choose.

We are not a fund with a clock. We deploy capital from a permanent vehicle into ventures, real estate, and operating companies where time is an asset rather than a constraint. We have no IPO timeline. No artificial exit pressure. No fund-end deadline.

Most of our investments are made through introductions from people we already know. The work begins with a conversation, not a pitch deck. If a relationship makes sense, we will find a way to make it work — and we will give it the time it needs.
