Questions

Frequently asked, honestly answered.

Is my data really mine? ↓
Yes. Every note is stored as plain markdown, locally by default, optionally synced to your own cloud storage. Export at any time — no vendor lock, no proprietary format. If we disappeared tomorrow, your work would still be readable by any text editor.
How is this different from Notion or Obsidian? ↓
Unlike Notion, Altitude is local-first, offline-native, and runs without loading database structures or heavy layouts. Unlike Obsidian, we optimize specifically for long-form prose and clear composition out of the box without requiring complex community plugin setups.
Do I need to know markdown formatting syntax? ↓
Not at all. You can write normally without any special characters. If you wish to use headers or lists, standard formatting shortcuts apply automatically behind the scenes to keep text neat.
Can I write completely offline? ↓
Yes. The app operates entirely from your local file system partition context. Cloud synchronization passes only execute once an active internet confirmation handshake occurs safely in your peripheral system.
